# AGENTS.md

## Applies to

This root card applies repository-wide unless a nearer `AGENTS.md` supplies a
narrower local delta.

## Role

`Agents-of-Abyss` is the constitutional and ecosystem center of AoA. It owns
ecosystem identity, charter, layer map, federation rules, program direction,
compact center registries, and center-local measurement over those surfaces.
It keeps the long arc legible without absorbing specialized implementation.

Skills, techniques, evals, memory, routing, KAG, playbooks, shared statistics,
roles, runtime behavior, and ToS authored meaning remain with their owner
repositories. Quest, checkpoint, progression, recurrence, or RPG language here
must not imply live runtime state, ledger authority, or unreviewable autonomy.

## Boundaries

- Follow the nearest nested `AGENTS.md` for every touched path. Root owns
  repository identity, owner boundaries, and route choice; nested cards own
  only their local contract, risk, stop-lines, and validation route.
- Authored owner sources define meaning. Generated registries, routing tables,
  compact indexes, adapters, exports, and reports are derived evidence or
  transport and must return to their source.
- Keep inherited chains within `config/agents_mesh.json` budgets. Large command
  matrices belong in a validated manifest, runner, or `VALIDATION.md`, not in
  prompt-visible cards.
- Do not hide semantic changes under "docs-only" or "metadata-only" wording,
  harden roadmap intent into implementation truth, or archive every package
  and session in root documents.

Contradictions among the charter, design, ecosystem map, layers, federation
rules, roadmap, or source-backed generated capsules are review-critical. So
are wrong-owner routes, unsupported public promises, and generated changes
without their source, builder, validator, and tests.

## Memory, decisions, and landing

Use `aoa_memo` for continuity or reviewed memory routing, while keeping proof,
runtime, KAG, role, and source meaning with their owners. Center-local
candidates move through `memo/`; durable reviewed memory is handed to
`aoa-memo`, not landed here as memory truth.

After a structural, ownership, route-law, validator-authority, public-contract,
or topology change, use `docs/decisions/AGENTS.md` to decide whether future
agents need a durable rationale. Update only the source, design, roadmap,
changelog, generated companions, mechanic ledgers, or neighboring owner routes
whose meaning actually changed.

The complete branch, PR, CI, and merge procedure is in `docs/RELEASING.md`;
`.github/AGENTS.md` owns GitHub-native files. If required status, review,
authority, or post-merge state cannot be observed, stop rather than infer it.

## Validation

Use `VALIDATION.md` for the narrowest applicable on-demand route and widen to
its full gate for release-facing work. Exact procedure belongs there, in the
validated mechanics route manifest and runner, or in a stronger owner
procedure. An unavailable required `aoa-stats` validator is a failed check,
not permission to call a partial gate green.

`docs/guardrails/ENTRY_SURFACE_VALIDATION_BASELINE.md` records the checked
entry-surface baseline without becoming executable authority.

## Closeout

Closeout names changed center surfaces, any shifted owner boundary, generated
parity, checks run and skipped, remaining risk, decision-review result, and the
next owner route. Keep local validation, CI, review, merge, publication,
runtime use, and owner acceptance as separate claims.

# Governance Polis Contract

## Center boundary

This part owns polis governance, constitution runtime boundary, council model,
votes, vetoes, appeals, stays, quarantine, disputes, precedents, authority
resolver language, and the constitutional C25 operator-decision envelope.

## Stronger owner split

`Agents-of-Abyss` owns center law; runtime enforcement and proof stay with stronger owners.

## Allowed outputs

- decision table, policy registry route, appeal or stay route, precedent index hint, and constitution-runtime stop-line.
- A refs-only `OperatorDecisionPacket` that binds the sole operator's
  `approve`, `reject`, `defer`, `narrow`, or `quarantine` decision to an exact
  owner-resolved artifact manifest.
- Center-side request packets, compatibility notes, and stop-lines.
- Inert projections that explicitly name the owner-local gate required before activation.

## Must not claim

- Live workspace runtime or runtime enforcement.
- Hidden memory sovereignty.
- Owner-local activation or acceptance.
- Proof verdicts before the proof owner lands evidence.
- Hidden precedent ledger or unreviewed authority resolver.
- AI final-decision authority, AI authority widening, or an unseparated
  evidence/authority review run.
- A decision when the exact artifact manifest is unresolved or mismatched.
- Automatic effect, production admission, payload meaning, memory semantics,
  routing, or proof verdict from the C25 envelope.
- ToS-authored meaning from an AoA center bridge.

# AGENTS.md

## Applies to

This card applies to `mechanics/method-growth/` and every nested path under
that scope until a nearer `AGENTS.md` narrows the lane.

## Read before editing


## Boundaries

- Do not use this lane to override owner-local truth, generated-source
  boundaries, sibling-repo authority, release validation contracts, proof
  verdicts, memory canon, runtime activation, or ToS-authored meaning.
- Do not mint `candidate_ref` or `object_ref` in the center.
  `aoa-skills` and the final owner repository own those stages.
- Do not restore a mandatory federation-wide seed intermediary. If an owner
  keeps `seed_ref` for compatibility, it remains owner-local metadata.
- Keep active parts light and route-shaped. Put deeper doctrine in `docs/`,
  historical lineage in `PROVENANCE.md`, and owner-local requests in
  `OWNER_REQUESTS.md`.
- If a real owner-local landing request changes, update
  `mechanics/owner-request-queue.json`, rebuild its generated companion, and
  keep the package request doc synchronized.

## Closeout


If `PROVENANCE.md` was consulted, name only the relevant lineage bridge or
receipt section. Do not enumerate donor or historical sources unless the task
specifically audited that evidence in depth.

## Role

Method-growth owns the center route from repeated work into donor refinement,
candidates, seeds, owner landings, pruning, methods, proofs, memory, derived
summaries, and closeout posture.

It does not own final object truth for specialized repositories.

## Source Surfaces

- `README.md`: package entry and route.
- `DIRECTION.md`: current active mechanic direction.
- `PARTS.md`: active functioning-part map.
- `parts/`: concise active method-growth contracts.
- `OWNER_MAP.md`: method-growth owner boundary and stronger-owner split.

- `ROADMAP.md`: current and next method-growth contour.
- `LANDING_LOG.md`: checked method-growth landing ledger.
- `PROVENANCE.md`: controlled bridge to lineage and source accounting.
- `docs/`: detailed doctrine and support notes.

## Post-change route review

After any method-growth change, check whether the next agent can start from the
current inherited `AGENTS.md` card, then `DIRECTION.md`, `PARTS.md`, and the
relevant active part without opening donor history or sibling owner surfaces.

Check whether the move changed:

- `DIRECTION.md`: current method-growth posture or route emphasis.
- `PARTS.md`: active part boundary, refinement route, or pruning shape.
- `OWNER_MAP.md`: owner boundary, stop-line, or handoff target.
- `OWNER_REQUESTS.md` and `mechanics/owner-request-queue.json`: skill, seed,
  proof, memory, KAG, stats, routing, or owner-local landing asks.
- `ROADMAP.md`: future route pressure or unresolved growth contour.
- `LANDING_LOG.md`: a checked landing or planted contract.
- `PROVENANCE.md`: lineage bridge, receipt route, or archive map.
- `mechanics/registry.json` and generated indexes: card-facing route, owner
  boundary, validation refs, or public summaries.

Only update a surface when its meaning moved. Leave it untouched when the
change does not affect its job.
